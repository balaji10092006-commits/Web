About

I spent the first two years of my career fixing things I'd built the wrong way the first time.

That's not a complaint — it's the fastest way I know to learn what "accessible" actually means in practice. Now I lead frontend builds where accessibility is a requirement from the first wireframe, not a pass at the end. I care about the same things a good print typesetter cares about: order, hierarchy, and making sure nothing is lost when the medium changes.

Outside of client work, I maintain a small open-source library of accessible form patterns and mentor two developers transitioning into frontend roles.

Experience

    Senior Frontend Developer, Northbridge Transit Co-op

  2. 2022 — 2024

    Frontend Developer, Fielding & Co.

  3. 2021 — 2022

    Learned semantic HTML the hard way: rebuilding a client's <div>-only marketing site after a failed accessibility audit.
