How I work

Semantic first

Native <button>, <nav>, and <fieldset> elements before a single line of ARIA. Less JavaScript, fewer edge cases.

Tested with real tools

Every interface is walked through with a keyboard only, then with VoiceOver or NVDA, before it's called done.

Built to hold contrast

Color pairings are checked against WCAG AA at minimum, and focus states are never invisible.
